{# The mark: five bars of ordinary traffic, one of them cut in two. The gap is the product. It is simultaneously traffic being shaped, an anomaly separated from its baseline, and the enforcement threshold drawn as an absence rather than a line. Geometric rather than a squiggle, so it survives 16px; the floating tip gives an asymmetric silhouette that survives being one colour. It is also honest about what the product measures — heights, per source, against each other. Not a shield, not a padlock, not a globe with arcs. Inline SVG because `default-src 'self'` reaches no external origin and an icon font would be a second request for one glyph. Rects rather than the equivalent path data: shorter, and the rounded corners come from rx. #} {% macro mark(size=20, cls="") %} {% endmacro %} {# The wordmark is inline SVG mark + HTML text, never outlined letterforms. "AI" is dropped from the displayed lockup deliberately: it is the least differentiated word available in 2026, and this product's actual differentiator is the two that follow.
