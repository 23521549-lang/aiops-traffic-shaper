What “unusual” currently means for your site.

We slow an IP when its behaviour is 4σ outside your normal, and block it at 5σ. For your current model that works out to an anomaly score below {{ "%.3f"|format(thresholds.slow) }} (slowed) or {{ "%.3f"|format(thresholds.block) }} (blocked).
